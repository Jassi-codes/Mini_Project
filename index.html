About Laboratory Services

Our Clinical Laboratory offers pathology and diagnostic services including blood tests, urine tests, thyroid profiling, liver and kidney function tests, lipid profiles, and diabetes screening. Our facility uses modern laboratory equipment and trained laboratory technicians to support reliable testing.

Pathology Tips

  • Stay hydrated before giving a blood sample, unless your doctor advises otherwise.
  • Follow fasting instructions provided by your healthcare professional.
  • Carry your doctor's prescription when required.
  • Collect reports on time and discuss them with your doctor.
