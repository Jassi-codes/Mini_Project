Common Test Abbreviations

CBC - Complete Blood Count; measures several components of blood.

HbA1c - Reflects average blood glucose over approximately three months.

LFT - Liver Function Test.

KFT - Kidney Function Test.

ECG - Electrocardiogram; records the electrical activity of the heart.
