Test Details - Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Test Name: Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: Used to evaluate overall health and help detect a range of conditions, including anaemia and infection.

Sample Required: A blood sample collected in an appropriate EDTA tube.

Preparation: Special preparation is often not required. Follow the instructions given by your healthcare provider.

Reference Range: Hemoglobin: 13-17 g/dL (Male), 12-15 g/dL (Female)

Report Time: Often available on the same day; actual timing depends on the laboratory.

Important Instructions

  • Inform the technician about current medicines when relevant.
  • Follow the sample collection instructions.
  • Fasting is not usually required for CBC, but some other tests may require it.

Note: This page is educational. Interpret results with a qualified healthcare professional.
